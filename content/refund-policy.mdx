# Refund & Cancellation Policy

At RRC Kitchen, we strive to ensure our customers are satisfied with their orders. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds and cancellations are processed.

## 1. Order Cancellations
- **Before Preparation:** You may cancel your order for a full refund if the kitchen has not yet started preparing it.
- **During Preparation:** Orders cannot be canceled once preparation has begun. If you choose to cancel at this stage, no refund will be issued.

## 2. Order Issues & Refunds
We will issue a full or partial refund in the following scenarios:
- **Incorrect Items:** If you receive an item different from what you ordered.
- **Missing Items:** If an item from your order is missing upon delivery.
- **Quality Issues:** If the food delivered does not meet our quality standards (e.g., spoiled, improperly cooked). Please provide photographic evidence within 2 hours of delivery.

## 3. Refund Processing
- Approved refunds will be processed back to the original method of payment.
- Please allow 3-5 business days for the refund to reflect in your account, depending on your bank or payment provider.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have an issue with your order, please contact our support team immediately through the Live Chat widget or email support@rrckitchen.com with your order details.
