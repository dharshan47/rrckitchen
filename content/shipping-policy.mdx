# Shipping Policy

This Shipping Policy governs the delivery and shipping of orders placed through RRC Kitchen.

## 1. Delivery Areas
We currently deliver to select neighborhoods. Please enter your address during checkout to verify if we deliver to your location.

## 2. Delivery Times
- **Standard Delivery:** Orders are typically delivered within 45-60 minutes from the time of order confirmation.
- **Scheduled Delivery:** You can schedule deliveries up to 24 hours in advance.

## 3. Delivery Charges
Delivery fees are calculated based on your distance from our kitchen. The exact delivery fee will be displayed at checkout before you confirm your order.

## 4. Order Tracking
Once your order is dispatched, you can track it in real-time through the Orders section of your account.

## 5. Tiffin Carrier Returns
In our commitment to sustainability and authentic home-cooked experiences, we deliver meals in reusable tiffin carriers.
- **Pickup Scheduling:** Our delivery partner will schedule a convenient time to return and pick up the empty tiffin carriers from your location.
- **Customer Responsibility:** Customers are required to return the tiffin carriers in a clean and undamaged condition. Failure to return the carriers may result in additional charges to your account.

## 6. Delays
While we strive to deliver on time, external factors like severe weather or traffic may cause delays. In such cases, we will notify you promptly.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about your delivery, please contact our support team through the Live Chat widget or email support@rrckitchen.com.
